HOW TO DRIVE

You're driving from Oberlaa toward Leopoldau. The train starts with its doors open.

W or ↑Hold to speed up
S or ↓Hold to slow down
DOpen or close doors at a station
A / ESelect left / right tracks near switches
SPACEEmergency brake
HTurn cabin headlights on/off
PPause/resume

Station challenge: stop within 12 metres of your next platform, reach 0 km/h, press D, wait for passengers, then press D to close the doors. You cannot drive away with open doors. Missing a platform loses points but never ends your journey.
